**A plan's `files:` is now the ticket's write scope, and the pass enforces it.** Until now nothing checked a cook's diff against its plan, and two cooks whose plans overlapped were caught only when the second PR conflicted. `kitchen.sh scope [station-N]` compares the station's changed files with the plan's `files:` (exact paths, globs, or a folder ending in `/`) and prints what is inside and what is outside; it exits 1 when anything is outside, or when no plan names the ticket. The record always travels with the PR: the plan, the ticket's journal and checklist, and the INDEX files are inside, and so are the globs under a new, optional `scope: always_inside:` in `.claude/brigade.md` (this repo lists its changelog fragment there; the plugin itself names no repo file). The taster runs it first and every outside path is a numbered finding; `review-task` lists the result in the checklist; `create-plan` and `implement` say a cook widens `files:` on purpose, with a line of why under Decisions → How, never by drift. The budget's debrief gains `scope-drift`, a `repo` rule that fires when a pass found a path outside the plan.

`kitchen.sh scope --overlap` is how the sous sees two scopes that share a path: it compares every station's plan and in-flight diff with every other station's and with every ready ticket's plan (else the ticket's own `files:`), and prints each collision as ticket (station, stage) × ticket with the paths, each side `planned` or `building`. `--ticket <ID>[,<ID>]` names a candidate a tracker rail can't list. Contracts 2, 4, 8, 9 and 11 and the sous-chef skill say so.

**What you do:** restart the sous and every cook so they load the new skills, and keep `files:` honest in your plans. To keep a changelog fragment (or any file every PR carries) inside the scope, add `scope:` with `always_inside: ["changelog.d/<id>-*.md"]` to `.claude/brigade.md`; `<id>` is the ticket's key (`92`, `bklg-004`).
