level: minor

**The sous no longer relays what a script can.** Most of the sous's turns were relays: a cook's check-in, a status line, a claim, a move to `in-progress`, a move to `rfr`, a `[heard]` back. Those are now script. A cook claims and moves its own briefed ticket with `kitchen.sh rail claim` and `kitchen.sh rail move` (the sous records the brief first, in the turn it sends the `[brief]`, with `kitchen.sh rail brief <ID> station-N`); the script refuses a ticket nobody briefed, a ticket someone else holds (a claim is never overwritten), and any move a cook may not make. A markdown rail is written as a commit built on `origin/<trunk>`, so the sous's checkout is never touched; a GitHub rail goes through `gh project item-edit`, as `/bk:rail` does. A cook reports its state with `kitchen.sh here` (a file in its station, shown under "The cooks" in `kitchen.sh board`); only a *free* cook still sends one `[check-in]` line, because a file cannot wake an idle sous. Contract 6's table now marks every message kind **sous**, **script** or **cook**.

The budget can now show the cost: `kitchen.sh budget --ticket <ID>` (and `--json`, and the ledger row) counts the sous's turns by the kind of message that woke them, and the debrief has two new rules, `relay-heavy` (more than half the sous's turns on a ticket were relays) and `ship-rounds` (a ticket pushed again after its first `[served]`). The budget also reads ticket ids in the rail's own form now, so a GitHub rail's `#13` and `issue-13-…` branch and a jira key get their spend, phases, asks and CI minutes (it showed "Tickets (none)" before). Closes #65 and #59.

**What you do:** restart the sous and every cook so they load the new skills. The sous runs `kitchen.sh rail brief` with each `[brief]`; if it forgets, a cook's claim is refused with a message saying so, and the cook falls back to the old `[rail]` message, so nothing stalls.
