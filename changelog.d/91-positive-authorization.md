A cook can now commit, push or open a pull request only when the sous has granted that action to its station: the sous writes the grant with `kitchen.sh grant <station> commit push open-pr` as it sends `[go]`, takes it away at `[served]`, and `done` and `clear` remove it too. `merge` is never granted. The plugin's hook refuses a cook's `git commit`, `git push` and `gh pr create` (and `git merge`, `pull`, `rebase`, `cherry-pick`, `revert` and `am` unless `--ff-only`) without the grant, a push to trunk, a force push, `gh pr merge`, `git reset --hard` and `git clean -x` whatever is granted, and a cook's edit of `.claude/check.log`, `.claude/procs`, `.claude/served` or the grants. You need do nothing: the sous does the granting. A cook that hits the refusal sends `[gate] <ID> no grant on record` and waits.
